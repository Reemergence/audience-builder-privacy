Audience Acquisition Builder privacy

Effective October 13, 2026. This private local installation combines Creator Studio and the YouTube MP4 downloader. Its operator controls the Windows computer and saved data.

Data and purpose

The app stores account/session records, source URLs and recordings, transcripts, edits, rendered files, permission declarations, job histories and publication receipts on this computer. Speech transcription and clip selection run locally. Media and project files are ordinary local files; they are not encrypted by this app.

Connecting Google requests YouTube upload and read-only access. The app reads channel identifiers and names to verify @Re3mergence, uploads your authorized finished videos and metadata, and reads their processing status and visibility to verify publication. OAuth client credentials and access/refresh tokens are encrypted for the current Windows user. Channel and publication metadata remain in the local database.

Sharing

Google receives authorization requests and tokens, selected video files and upload metadata when you connect or publish. YouTube receives source requests when you import a YouTube URL. Publicly published videos and their metadata are visible on YouTube. This Audience Builder pipeline does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising or model training. Google handles information under Google's Privacy Policy.

Control and retention

Use Cancel future work to stop pending production or posting. Cancelling does not remove a video already uploaded to YouTube; manage that video in YouTube Studio. Saved projects, files, credentials and receipts persist until removed; copies can remain in local backups or diagnostic import folders. Delete projects using the app's project controls. Deleting the local app account removes its database credentials and schedules project-file cleanup; running jobs must first stop.

Revoke Google's authorization through Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized API access; local saved records can remain. For local data removal or privacy questions, contact the operator of this installation. Google's testing access may require reconnection; the app reports connection failures rather than claiming publication.

This app uses YouTube API Services. See YouTube Terms of Service and Google API Services User Data Policy.
